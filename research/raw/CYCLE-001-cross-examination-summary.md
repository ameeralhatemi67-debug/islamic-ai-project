### DOCKET TRIAL 1: CX-DOCK-01
- **Target Problem Cards:** `P-002` (Fatwa Paralysis), `P-301` (Fiqh Context-Flattening)
- **Target AI Opportunities:** `AI-OP-002`, `AI-OP-011`
- **Assigned Challenge IDs:** `RT-P-002`, `RT-P-301`
- **Core Substantive Dispute:** 
  > *Does conversational slot-filling to gather situational variables and deliver tailored legal rulings constitute automated Ifta (Tahqiq al-Manat al-Khass), breaching the supreme constitutional "No Autonomous Mufti" rule? Furthermore, does presenting four parallel madhhab columns alleviate or exacerbate clinical religious scrupulosity (al-waswas al-qahri)?*

#### 1. Claim Freeze (Original Claims Under Attack)
- *P-002 Claim:* Conversational intent classification and slot-filling parses lay descriptions of lifestyle dilemmas (student loans, employment benefits) into legal categories and retrieves matched 4-madhhab passages from the *Kuwaiti Fiqh Encyclopedia*, directly resolving conflicting fatwa paralysis and alleviating *waswas* (religious OCD).
- *P-301 Claim:* Conversational slot-filling prompts users for missing situational variables (madhhab, health condition, travel status) and retrieves multi-madhhab passages, providing an automated fiqh guardrail that prevents single-school algorithmic monopolies.

#### 2. Summary of Council Debate
- **Challenger Attack (Agent 07 — Red Team & Agent 04 — Islamic Trust):**
  - *The Theological Red Line:* In classical Islamic jurisprudence (*Usul al-Fiqh*), issuing a fatwa is not merely looking up an abstract rule in an encyclopedia; it is evaluating the specific, complex facts of an individual (*Tahqiq al-Manat al-Khass*) and determining whether hardship (*Mashaqqah*) or necessity (*Darurah*) justifies a legal concession (*Rukhsa*). An algorithm that prompts for personal circumstances and outputs a tailored ruling is performing automated *Ifta* by proxy. Classical consensus (*Ijma'*) establishes that algorithms lack legal capacity (*Faqd al-Ahliyyah*).
  - *The Clinical Scrupulosity Paradox:* In psychiatric and pastoral reality, individuals suffering from religious scrupulosity (*al-waswas al-qahri*, `E01-003`) suffer from an acute intolerance of ambiguity. Showing a scrupulous believer four contradictory columns (Hanafi allows, Shafi'i forbids, Hanbali dislikes, Maliki qualifies) accelerates obsessive checking, doubt, and panic ("Am I committing a major sin if I pick the lenient one?").
  - *Corpus Mismatch:* The Kuwaiti Fiqh Encyclopedia digests pre-modern case law (camels, dirhams, agricultural bartering). Using an LLM to map modern fintech (crypto staking, index fund rollovers) to classical passages produces absurd, dangerous analogical misapplications (*Qiyas Ma'al-Fariq*).
- **Defender Rebuttal (Agent 06 — AI Architect & Agent 01 — Muslim User Researcher):**
  - Agent 06 argued that the LLM is NOT generating new rulings; it is performing extractive retrieval from accredited classical texts. The model is constrained by JSON schemas, and high-stakes personal status queries (divorce, custody, inheritance, takfir) are intercepted by hard deterministic abstention gates.
  - Agent 01 argued that lay Muslims already Google these dilemmas and land on IslamQA.info (#1 SEO), which enforces monolithic Hanbali strictness, erases legitimate school divergence, and triggers deep panic. Presenting the reality of *Ikhtilaf* proves to the believer that legitimate diversity exists, reducing single-source anxiety.

#### 3. Director Adjudication & Ruling (Agent 08)
- **Status:** **RESOLVED — CLAIM NARROWED & REVISED (P0 CONCEDED)**
- **Adjudicated Findings:**
  1. *Theological Red Line Upheld:* The constitutional prohibition (`AGENTS.md` Sec 6; `source-policy.md` Sec 15) is absolute. Conversational slot-filling that diagnoses an individual's personal life situation to deliver an actionable personal ruling crosses into automated *Ifta*. Software cannot practice *Tahqiq al-Manat al-Khass*.
  2. *Clinical Scrupulosity Claim Stripped:* The claim that 4-column comparative matrices cure clinical *waswas* is **DISCONFIRMED**. Clinical scrupulosity requires behavioral therapy and unambiguous pastoral direction. All claims of clinical efficacy are formally excised from `P-002`.
  3. *Reclassification as Educational Comparative Archive:* `P-002` and `P-301` are stripped of all personal advisory logic. They survive strictly as **Educational Comparative Jurisprudence Archives** that display historical school positions on abstract, theoretical, or pedagogical topics (e.g., "What are the historical conditions of wudu according to the four schools?").
  4. *Mandatory Human Scholar Gate:* Any first-person narrative ("I did X, am I sinful?") or personal status query triggers an unconditional, hardcoded refusal directing the user to certified human muftis and institutional fatwa councils (`[NO AUTONOMOUS FATWA PERMITTED]`).
  5. *Modern Issue Sourcing:* Modern fintech and medical questions cannot be answered by prompting classical texts; they must strictly reference modern institutional resolutions (OIC Fiqh Academy, ECFR, AAOIFI).

### DOCKET TRIAL 2: CX-DOCK-02
- **Target Problem Card:** `P-003` (Quran Mutashabihat Navigation)
- **Target AI Opportunity:** `AI-OP-003`
- **Assigned Challenge ID:** `RT-P-003`
- **Core Substantive Dispute:**
  > *Should P-003 be stripped of all AI claims, recognizing that offline suffix trees, LCS, and SQLite solve 95% of verse matching with zero hallucination and zero compute? Is the proposed on-device ASR active-recall drill a fragile gimmick?*

#### 1. Claim Freeze (Original Claims Under Attack)
- *P-003 / AI-OP-003 Claim:* While deterministic algorithms solve 95% of verse diffing, an on-device acoustic speech recognition (ASR) phoneme alignment drill is necessary to track oral recitation during active recall, alerting the memorizer when they confuse verbal twin verses.

#### 2. Summary of Council Debate
- **Challenger Attack (Agent 07 — Red Team):**
  - *Total Non-AI Baseline Dominance:* The Quranic text is fixed, immutable, and finite (6,236 verses, ~77,430 words). Longest Common Subsequence (LCS), suffix trees, and Levenshtein string diffing can precompute and index every verbal variation across the entire Quran in under 2 seconds into a lightweight SQLite database. Split-screen visual diffing, synchronized audio playback, and classical exegesis mapping require **zero AI, zero cloud compute, and carry zero hallucination risk**.
  - *ASR Fragility on Tajweed:* Acoustic speech recognition on non-native Arabic Quranic recitation suffers from high word error rates. Phonetic nuances of Tajweed (*ghunnah*, *ikhfa*, *qalqalah*, *madd*) and canonical variant readings (*Qira'at*) cause acoustic models to falsely flag correct recitations as errors, infuriating reciters and destroying user trust.
  - *Disruption of Spatial Memory:* Traditional Huffadh memorize via spatial visual retention of the standard 15-line Medina Mushaf (King Fahd Complex). Dynamic responsive split-screen diffs dismantle this spatial landmark memory, creating cognitive friction during live recitation from memory.
- **Defender Rebuttal (Agent 06 — AI Architect & Agent 01 — Muslim User Researcher):**
  - Agent 06 argued that on-device phoneme alignment against a *known, closed reference text* is fundamentally easier than open-ended ASR. It does not transcribe arbitrary speech; it merely checks whether token A (*tatawwu'an*) or token B (*tatawwa'a*) was uttered, enabling hands-free solo revision.
  - Agent 01 stressed the intense anxiety Huffadh experience during Ramadan Taraweeh prayers when they get stuck on verbal twins, arguing that speech drills provide critical motor-auditory reinforcement.

#### 3. Director Adjudication & Ruling (Agent 08)
- **Status:** **RESOLVED — CORE RECLASSIFIED AS NON-AI (P0 CONCEDED)**
- **Adjudicated Findings:**
  1. *Non-AI Dominance Confirmed:* The Red Team is correct. Deterministic computer science completely dominates the problem space. The core product MUST be classified as a **Deterministic Relational Quranic Syntax & Diff Engine**. 
  2. *ASR Demoted to Optional Experimental Add-on:* The claim that ASR is essential is **REJECTED**. The core value proposition survives 100% without machine learning. On-device acoustic phoneme alignment is retained strictly as an unbundled, optional experimental secondary module, with prominent disclaimers regarding Tajweed limitations.
  3. *Spatial Layout Preservation Mandated:* To protect traditional memorizers' photographic memory, the UI must provide a fixed 15-line Medina Mushaf overlay mode rather than unconstrained fluid reflow.
  4. *Balaghah Sourcing Locked:* Exegetical explanations for verbal differences must be strictly cited from classical works (*Durrat al-Tanzil*, *Al-Burhan*); generative AI speculation on divine word choice is strictly forbidden.

### DOCKET TRIAL 3: CX-DOCK-03
- **Target Problem Card:** `P-100` (Solitary Salah Learning Overload)
- **Target AI Opportunity:** `AI-OP-004`
- **Assigned Challenge ID:** `RT-P-100`

#### 1. Claim Freeze (Original Claims Under Attack)
- *P-100 / AI-OP-004 Claim:* On-device computer vision pose tracking (MediaPipe 33-point skeletal landmarking via a propped smartphone) dynamically tracks prayer postures (*Qiyam*, *Ruku*, *Sujud*), synchronizing audio cues to the convert's physical movement while preserving privacy via edge processing.

#### 2. Summary of Council Debate
- **Challenger Attack (Agent 07 — Red Team):**
  - *Physical Garment Occlusion:* MediaPipe fundamentally relies on visible joint contrast (hips, knees, ankles). Islamic prayer mandates loose-fitting clothing (*'Awrah*): women pray in flowing abayas, long prayer skirts (*isdal*), and head coverings; men often wear loose *thobes*. Loose fabric completely obscures knee flexion and hip angles from computer vision.
  - *Prostration (*Sujud*) Collapse:* During Sujud, the human body folds into a compressed, self-occluding shape where 2D/3D skeletal models lose tracking entirely.
  - *Optical Space Constraints:* Propping a phone on a desk in a small convert bedroom cannot achieve the 6-to-8-foot optical clearance required to frame both standing and prostration in a single frame without severe lens distortion.
  - *Destruction of Khushu' & Surveillance Anxiety:* Solitary prayer demands absolute inner stillness and surrender (*Khushu'*). Pointing a camera lens at a vulnerable new convert (especially Muslim women in their homes) induces acute performance anxiety and surveillance self-consciousness, fundamentally corrupting the spiritual state of worship.
  - *Ritual Invalidation (*Harakah Kathirah*):* If the camera lags or misclassifies a posture, the user is forced to glance at the screen or adjust the phone. Three consecutive extraneous movements or turning away from the Qiblah invalidates the prayer entirely (*Fasad al-Salah*).
- **Defender Rebuttal (Agent 06 — AI Architect & Agent 02 — New Muslim Researcher):**
  - Agent 02 highlighted the acute pain of first-year converts: trying to synchronize 17 postures, Arabic phonetics, and sequences causes massive cognitive overload, forcing converts to pray with cheat sheets on the floor or pause YouTube videos with wet wudu hands.
  - Agent 06 argued that MediaPipe could be coarse-grained into general posture bounding envelopes (standing vs bowing vs floor prostration) rather than exact knee angles, and emphasized that 100% on-device processing guarantees privacy.

#### 3. Director Adjudication & Ruling (Agent 08)
- **Status:** **RESOLVED — CAMERA CV PERMANENTLY TERMINATED (P0 CONCEDED)**
- **Adjudicated Findings:**
  1. *Physical & Spiritual Invalidation of Camera CV:* The Red Team's arguments regarding garment occlusion, floor geometry, and bedroom focal lengths are physically decisive. Furthermore, introducing a camera into private prayer violates the sacred contemplative nature of worship. **Camera-based computer vision pose tracking is PERMANENTLY TERMINATED AND ABANDONED.**
  2. *Architectural Pivot to Adaptive Hands-Free Audio & Motion Coach:* The genuine problem of cognitive overload and lack of gradualism (*Tadrij*) survives completely. The solution is reframed as an **Adaptive Hands-Free Audio Coach**:
     - *Primary Mode:* Voice Activity Detection (VAD) / whisper detection (detecting completions of "Allahu Akbar" or silent pauses) delivering whispered prompt cues through a single wireless Bluetooth earbud.
     - *Secondary Non-Optical Motion (Optional):* Low-friction device orientation (accelerometer/gyroscope in pocket or smartwatch wrist-tilt) or a discreet single-tap Bluetooth ring/floor pedal.
  3. *Prophetic Tadrij Curriculum Upheld:* The 4-tier gradualism curriculum (*Sunan Abi Dawud 858*) remains the core pedagogical innovation, allowing converts to progress naturally from basic tasbeeh to full adult prayer without screen distraction.
