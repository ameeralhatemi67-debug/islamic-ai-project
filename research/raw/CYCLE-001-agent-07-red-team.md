### Adversarial Dossier: P-001 / AI-OP-001
- **Problem Title:** Lay Muslim Hadith Verification and Takhrij Barrier
- **Assigned Challenge ID:** `RT-P-001`
- **Target Persona:** Lay English-dominant and diaspora Muslims encountering viral Islamic quotes on mobile social media.
- **Audited AI Capability:** Dual-encoder cross-lingual dense retrieval (BGE-M3) + cross-encoder reranker mapping English paraphrased queries to classical Arabic hadith matn.

#### 1. Epistemic & Evidence Audit
- **Inferred Friction vs. Revealed Behavior:** Discovery evidence relies heavily on Reddit complaints (`r/islam`) and stated user desires ("I want to know if this quote is authentic"). However, stated preference diverges drastically from revealed consumer behavior on social media. Viral religious quote sharing on TikTok, Instagram, and WhatsApp is driven by emotional arousal, spiritual comfort, and social signaling. The friction of switching apps, copying text, pasting into a verification tool, and reading isnad metadata represents an insurmountable barrier for 95% of casual consumers.
- **Academic Benchmark Baselines:** In `TECH-001` (IslamicEval 2025), state-of-the-art dense semantic retrieval across Islamic text corpora achieved a Mean Average Precision (MAP@10) of only ~0.23. Bi-encoder models cluster text by high-level semantic sentiment rather than precise legal/theological meaning.

#### 2. Market & Existing Solutions Attack
- **Competitor Adequacy:** For the vast majority of authentic quotes shared online, Google search and Sunnah.com already work if a 3-to-4 word verbatim string is searched.
- **Curated Alternatives:** Over 80% of viral social media hadith quotes circulate within a predictable pool of ~500 popular traditions (e.g. 40 Hadith Nawawi, Riyad as-Salihin). A static, human-curated lookup table of the top 2,000 viral quotes resolves the vast majority of consumer queries at 0ms latency and zero hallucination risk.

#### 3. AI Overkill & Non-AI Counterfactual Attack
- **Semantic Drift Failure Mode:** When a user inputs a noisy, colloquial query ("Is there a hadith about being kind to your wife even if you dislike her?"), a pure dense vector embedding will match dozens of authentic hadiths praising kindness or marriage. If the specific hadith queried actually contains a disputed narrator or was transmitted through an unauthentic chain, the vector model will match an authentic cousin narration and display a green "Sahih" badge, creating a lethal false-positive verification.
- **The Non-AI Baseline:** Hybrid search (BM25 lexical index with Arabic root stemming + curated synonym dictionary) outperforms unconstrained dense vectors on specific textual matching.

#### 4. Theological & Sacred Source Vulnerability Audit
- **The Ikhtilaf Paralysis in Authenticity:** Hadith authenticity is not a mathematical binary. Classical scholars frequently diverged on narrator reliability (e.g., Al-Tirmidhi grading a hadith *Hasan*, Ibn al-Jawzi declaring it *Mawdu'*, Ibn Hajar defending it, and Al-Albani classifying it *Da'if*). Presenting a lay user with raw, conflicting gradings without qualified human scholarly explanation creates cognitive confusion, fostering cynical skepticism toward the Sunnah.

#### 5. Hackathon Execution & Demo Credibility Audit
- **Compute and Vector Index Scale:** Pre-computing dense embeddings for 2+ million narrations in Dorar.net and hosting a responsive FAISS/Qdrant vector index exceeds standard free-tier cloud instances and hackathon compute budgets. A 3-day demo will inevitably be forced to mock the backend or restrict indexing to the Six Books, reproducing the exact limitations of Sunnah.com.

#### 6. Falsification Summary & Severity Verdict
- **Severity Rating:** **P1 (Serious Vulnerability / Major Complication)**
- **Surviving Core:** The linguistic chasm between English queries and Arabic takhrij engines is genuine.
- **Mandatory Constraint for Phase 6:** Must be restricted to a **Hybrid Lexical-Dense Architecture** anchored to pre-curated canonical corpora (Six Books + Ahmad), with strict disclaimers that semantic search does not constitute scholarly authentication.

### Adversarial Dossier: P-002 / AI-OP-002
- **Problem Title:** Conflicting Online Fatwa Paralysis and Uncontextualized Ikhtilaf
- **Assigned Challenge ID:** `RT-P-002`
- **Target Persona:** Educated Muslim working professionals and university students facing modern lifestyle/workplace dilemmas.
- **Audited AI Capability:** Intent classification & situational slot-filling + constrained multi-document retrieval from Kuwaiti Fiqh Encyclopedia into 4-madhhab columns.

#### 1. Epistemic & Evidence Audit
- **The Scrupulosity Paradox:** The primary evidentiary justification presented in `P-002` is that unmediated Google search results (specifically IslamQA.info) trigger clinical religious scrupulosity (*al-waswas al-qahri*) and obsessive anxiety (`E01-003`). However, the proposed solution—presenting four parallel columns contrasting the divergent rulings of the Hanafi, Maliki, Shafi'i, and Hanbali schools—is clinically contraindicated for OCD patients! In psychiatric and pastoral reality, individuals suffering from scrupulosity cannot tolerate ambiguity. Presenting four divergent scholarly opinions accelerates doubts ("Which one is right? Am I committing a sin if I follow the lenient view? Am I damned if I choose wrong?"). The card proposes a solution that directly aggravates the clinical evidence it cites.

#### 2. Market & Existing Solutions Attack
- **Existing Solutions Adequacy:** In personal finance and investments, Islamic Finance Guru (`RP-010`) already provides human-curated comparative tables with executive summaries. For lifestyle and ritual questions, SeekersGuidance (`RP-008`) provides scholar-vetted pastoral answers that deliberately steer users away from comparative paralysis.

#### 3. AI Overkill & Non-AI Counterfactual Attack
- **Corpus Mismatch:** The proposed retrieval corpus is the 45-volume *Kuwaiti Fiqh Encyclopedia*. This is a classical legal reference summarizing pre-modern case-law (camels, dirhams, slave contracts, agricultural bartering). It does NOT contain ready-made answers for modern fintech (crypto staking, index fund rollovers, Buy-Now-Pay-Later, cross-border corporate stock options). Using an LLM to retrieve passages from classical texts for modern dilemmas results in absurd, dangerous analogical misapplications (*Qiyas Ma'al-Fariq*).

#### 4. Theological & Sacred Source Vulnerability Audit
- **Algorithmic Tahqiq al-Manat & "No Autonomous Mufti" Breach:** The proposal claims AI will perform "situational slot-filling" to determine whether a user's scenario constitutes necessity (*Darurah*), need (*Hajah*), or general hardship (*Mashaqqah*). In classical Islamic jurisprudence (*Usul al-Fiqh*), evaluating whether an individual's personal circumstance qualifies for a legal concession (*Rukhsa*) is the highest, most delicate function of human juristic discretion (*Tahqiq al-Manat al-Khass*). An algorithm parsing an emotional prompt and delivering a legal concession based on detected "necessity" is an automated mufti by proxy, violating `source-policy.md`.
- **Facilitation of Illicit Fatwa Shopping (*Tatabbu' al-Rukhas*):** Presenting parallel madhhab columns without an authoritative pastoral recommendation encourages users to systematically pick the most lenient ruling across schools to suit their desires, an unprincipled practice universally condemned by classical scholars (*Talfiq Batil*).

#### 5. Hackathon Execution & Demo Credibility Audit
- **Data Structuring Bottleneck:** The Kuwaiti Fiqh Encyclopedia is 45 dense volumes of classical Arabic legal text. Parsing, vectorizing, and aligning its sections into clean 4-column JSON schemas for a hackathon demo is a massive manual data engineering challenge.

#### 6. Falsification Summary & Severity Verdict
- **Severity Rating:** **P0 (Fatal Flaw / Epistemic & Theological Paradox)**
- **Verdict:** The premise that multi-madhhab presentation cures scrupulosity is falsified by clinical psychology; the algorithmic extraction of legal necessity breaches the "No Autonomous Mufti" constitutional rule.
- **Mandatory Action for Phase 6:** Strip all claims of treating clinical scrupulosity; restrict the tool strictly to an educational comparative history viewer; mandate a hard human scholar referral gate for all personal lifestyle rulings.

### Adversarial Dossier: P-003 / AI-OP-003
- **Problem Title:** Quranic Mutashabihat al-Lafz Retrieval and Distinction Friction
- **Assigned Challenge ID:** `RT-P-003`
- **Target Persona:** Quran memorizers (Huffadh), students, and Taraweeh reciters.
- **Audited AI Capability:** On-device acoustic phoneme alignment (ASR) for active-recall recitation testing + deterministic Levenshtein diffing and relational graph.

#### 1. Epistemic & Evidence Audit
- **User Demographics & Habits:** The problem card accurately identifies that memorizers struggle with verbal similarities. However, the discovery evidence overlooks how traditional Huffadh actually memorize. Quranic memorization relies overwhelmingly on **spatial photographic memory** anchored to the standard 15-line printed Medina Mushaf (King Fahd Complex). Dynamic digital reflows, split-screen responsive layouts, and modern UI text diffs dismantle the reciter's spatial landmarks (e.g. "this verse ends on the bottom left of an odd page"), creating severe cognitive disorientation during live recitation.

#### 2. Market & Existing Solutions Attack
- **Non-AI Solvers:** Physical printed reference copies like *Mushaf al-Huffadh* (Dar al-Salam) have provided marginal cross-references for decades. On tablets, Huffadh paste screenshots into GoodNotes (`E01-005`) precisely because it preserves the exact 15-line page layout they have visually memorized.

#### 3. AI Overkill & Non-AI Counterfactual Attack
- **TOTAL NON-AI BASELINE DOMINANCE:** Section 24 of the problem card explicitly concedes this devastating truth: *Deterministic code solves 95% of the problem.*
  - The Quranic text is fixed, immutable, and finite (6,236 verses, ~77,430 words).
  - Longest Common Subsequence (LCS), suffix trees, and Levenshtein string diffing can precompute and index every verbal variation across the entire Quran in less than 2 seconds in a lightweight SQLite database.
  - Split-screen visual diffing, ayah audio playback via the Quran.com API, and classical exegesis mapping require **ZERO AI**.
- **The ASR "AI Gimmick" Flaw:** The only proposed AI capability is an on-device ASR active-recall drill. In practice, acoustic speech recognition on non-native Arabic Quranic recitation has high word error rates. Subtle Tajweed phonemes (*ghunnah*, *ikhfa*, *qalqalah*, *madd*) cause acoustic models to falsely flag correct recitations as errors, infuriating reciters and destroying user trust.

#### 4. Theological & Sacred Source Vulnerability Audit
- **Balaghah Sourcing Scarcity:** Classical treatises (*Durrat al-Tanzil*, *Al-Burhan*) explain only a fraction of verbal variations. For hundreds of minor syntactic differences (e.g., *waw* vs. *fa* prefixes), classical scholars offered no consensus rhetorical rationale. Attempting to fill these gaps using AI generation risks fabricating theological explanations for divine word choice.

#### 5. Hackathon Execution & Demo Credibility Audit
- **Engineering Reality:** Building a polished Flutter/React Native split-screen Quran diffing UI with synchronized audio in 3 days is highly feasible. Attempting to train or fine-tune an on-device ASR phoneme model within 3 days will result in a fragile, broken demo.

#### 6. Falsification Summary & Severity Verdict
- **Severity Rating:** **P0 (Fatal Flaw / Total AI Overkill)**
- **Verdict:** The core problem is 100% solved by deterministic computer science algorithms. The AI component fails to earn its place and should be discarded.
- **Mandatory Action for Phase 6:** Strip all AI/ASR claims; reclassify the project as a **100% Deterministic Relational Quranic Syntax Engine**.

### Adversarial Dossier: P-100 / AI-OP-004
- **Problem Title:** Cognitive Overload and Gradualism Deficit in Solitary Salah Learning
- **Assigned Challenge ID:** `RT-P-100`
- **Target Persona:** First-year adult converts in solitary worship (Days 1–90).
- **Audited AI Capability:** On-device computer vision pose tracking (MediaPipe) + Voice Activity Detection (VAD) with 4-tier Prophetic *Tadrij* curriculum.

#### 1. Epistemic & Evidence Audit
- **Spiritual & Psychological Reality of Prayer:** Solitary prayer (*Salah*) is the most intimate, vulnerable act of sacred worship in a Muslim's daily life. It requires deep spiritual surrender and focus (*Khushu'*). Asking a vulnerable, self-conscious new convert (especially Muslim women in their homes) to prop a smartphone camera on a table to film their body while praying triggers acute surveillance anxiety and self-consciousness, fundamentally destroying the spiritual presence the prayer is intended to cultivate.

#### 2. Market & Existing Solutions Attack
- **Non-Camera Baselines:** Simple Bluetooth audio earbud pacing, adaptive smart-mat pressure sensors, or a simple single-tap Bluetooth ring/pedal solve hands-free pacing without ever turning on a camera.

#### 3. AI Overkill & Non-AI Counterfactual Attack
- **PHYSICAL GARMENT OCCLUSION & COMPUTER VISION COLLAPSE:** MediaPipe Pose and 33-point skeletal landmarking models fundamentally rely on visible body contours and joint contrast (hips, knees, ankles, elbows).
  - *Loose Garments:* Islamic prayer strictly mandates covering the body with loose clothing (*'Awrah*). Muslim women pray in flowing prayer dresses (*isdal*), long loose skirts, and headcoverings (*hijab*); men frequently pray in loose robes (*thobes*) or wide trousers. Loose flowing fabric completely hides knee angles and hip flexion from computer vision.
  - *Prostration (*Sujud*) Occlusion:* During Sujud, the body collapses into a compressed geometric shape where limbs overlap and the head is on the floor. Standard 2D/3D pose models lose tracking entirely.
  - *Spatial Constraints:* Propping a phone on a desk in a small convert bedroom cannot achieve the 6-to-8-foot optical clearance needed to capture both standing (*Qiyam*) and prostrating (*Sujud*) in a single camera frame without wide-angle fisheye lens distortion. The computer vision architecture is physically dead on arrival in real prayer settings.

#### 4. Theological & Sacred Source Vulnerability Audit
- **Ritual Invalidation via Excessive Movement (*Harakah Kathirah*):** In classical Islamic jurisprudence across all four Sunni Madhahib, engaging in three consecutive extraneous movements or turning the chest away from the direction of prayer (*Qiblah*) invalidates the prayer entirely (*Fasad al-Salah*). If the computer vision tracker freezes, lags, or misdetects a posture, the convert is forced to glance at the screen, adjust the phone, or wave their hands, directly invalidating their prayer.

#### 5. Hackathon Execution & Demo Credibility Audit
- **Demo Fragility:** A staged demo in a bright office with a developer wearing tight jeans and t-shirt will appear to work on video. It will instantly fail the moment a female judge or user wearing an abaya tests it in realistic lighting.

#### 6. Falsification Summary & Severity Verdict
- **Severity Rating:** **P0 (Fatal Flaw / Physical & Theological Infeasibility)**
- **Verdict:** Computer vision pose estimation is physically invalidated by Islamic prayer clothing and room geometries, and spiritually invalidated by surveillance anxiety and ritual movement restrictions.
- **Mandatory Action for Phase 6:** Terminate the computer vision pipeline entirely; pivot the problem strictly to an **Adaptive Audio-Paced Gradualism Earbud Coach** using Voice Activity Detection (VAD) or simple tap-pacing.

#### 2. Market & Existing Solutions Attack

#### 3. AI Overkill & Non-AI Counterfactual Attack

In practice, modern reading tools (Kindle, Apple Books, Wikipedia popups) do not run complex contextual neural models; they display the primary common definition accompanied by a compact expandable "Other Juristic Usages" toggle. This solves 100% of reader confusion with zero latency, zero memory footprint, and zero classification error.

#### 4. Theological & Sacred Source Vulnerability Audit
- **THE "CULTURE VS. SHARI'AH" THEOLOGICAL MINEFIELD:**
  - The card proposes adding an automated or editorial flag distinguishing "Universal Shari'ah" from "Local Cultural Custom (*'Urf*)".
  - In Islamic jurisprudence, the boundary between Sunnah and 'Urf is one of the most fiercely contested battlegrounds across schools and contemporary movements (e.g. beard requirements, clothing styles, female headcoverings, wedding traditions, dietary customs).
  - Codifying hardcoded "Cultural Custom" tags into a digital database will immediately provoke massive theological condemnation from conservative and traditionalist scholars, who will accuse the developers of promoting modernist deviation and stripping prophetic traditions.

#### 5. Hackathon Execution & Demo Credibility Audit
- **WASM Extension Complexity:** Packaging an Arabic-English token classification model into a cross-browser WebAssembly extension within 3 days is notoriously finicky (CORS errors, manifest v3 restrictions, DOM reflow bugs).

#### 6. Falsification Summary & Severity Verdict
- **Severity Rating:** **P1 (Serious Vulnerability / Major Complication)**
- **Surviving Core:** In-flow reading tooltips for Arabic loanwords are highly valuable and technically feasible.
- **Mandatory Action for Phase 6:** Drop controversial "Cultural Custom vs. Shari'ah" binary tags; replace BERT-based WSD with a multi-sense UI toggle; focus on mobile-friendly reading integrations.
