### Cluster 1: Hadith Takhrij, Authentication & Provenance Verification
Four cards address prophetic reports, but their workflows and friction profiles operate in entirely different operational realities:

1. **`P-001` (Lay Consumer / Mobile Verification):**
   - *Persona:* General Muslim, youth, social media consumer.
   - *Trigger:* Encounters an inspirational, shocking, or politicized hadith graphic on Instagram, TikTok, or WhatsApp.
   - *Workflow:* Needs instant 30-second verification on a smartphone.
   - *Barrier:* Sunnah.com search is brittle to word inflections; Dorar.net is classical Arabic only; user cannot interpret technical hadith terminology (*Mursal*, *Munqati'*, *Da'if jiddan*).
   - *Consequence:* Either forwards fabricated quotes or succumbs to cynical skepticism toward prophetic traditions.

2. **`P-200` (Imam & Volunteer Khatib / Sermon Preparation Deadline):**
   - *Persona:* Community religious leader, volunteer Friday speaker.
   - *Trigger:* Drafting weekly 20-minute sermon under severe Thursday night deadline.
   - *Workflow:* Laptop drafting; searching for specific thematic narrations with historical context (*Sabab al-Wurud*).
   - *Barrier:* Needs to reconcile conflicting gradings across classical and modern scholars (e.g., Al-Tirmidhi vs. Ibn Hajar vs. Al-Albani) without getting bogged down in hours of isnad research.
   - *Consequence:* Repetitive sermon topics; relying on unvetted secondary booklets; severe vulnerability to quoting weak/fabricated narrations from the minbar.

3. **`P-300` (Digital Creator & Educator / AI Hallucination & Phantom Citations):**
   - *Persona:* Modern da'wah worker, video creator, curriculum author.
   - *Trigger:* Prompts a general-purpose LLM (ChatGPT, Claude) for an authentic hadith on a contemporary topic.
   - *Workflow:* Model generates a beautifully phrased, authoritative English quote with an invented citation ("Sahih al-Bukhari #4821").
   - *Barrier:* Autoregressive hallucination. The citation does not exist or points to an unrelated legal topic. The user spends 30–60 minutes hunting the "phantom" citation across databases.
   - *Consequence:* Direct injection of synthetic sacred speech into high-circulation digital publications and social videos.

4. **`P-302` (Academic Researcher & Publishing Editor / Cross-Corpus Disconnection):**
   - *Persona:* Madrasah graduate, Islamic university researcher, professional publisher.
   - *Trigger:* Systematic takhrij, tahqiq, or academic peer-review of a manuscript.
   - *Workflow:* High-intensity desktop research across 15+ browser tabs and desktop software suites.
   - *Barrier:* Structural siloing between Maktabah Shamela (Arabic desktop text), Dorar.net (authenticity database), and Sunnah.com (English translations). No cross-edition concordance; no isnad tree harmonization.
   - *Consequence:* 30 to 90 minutes spent authenticating a single citation; >60% verification abandonment in informal publishing.

*De-duplication Verdict:* **MAINTAIN AS 4 DISTINCT PROBLEMS.** They require completely different interfaces (mobile consumer vs. desktop sermon editor vs. hallucination guardrail vs. scholar research graph).

### Cluster 2: Conflicting Fatwas, Ikhtilaf & Jurisprudential Context
Three cards address legal rulings (*fiqh*), but reflect fundamentally different stages of human experience and technical failure:

1. **`P-002` (Lay Professional & Student / Search Portal Paralysis):**
   - *Persona:* Educated Muslim professional, university student.
   - *Trigger:* Personal lifestyle, workplace, or financial dilemma (e.g., mortgage, student loans, cosmetic procedures, crypto).
   - *Workflow:* Unmediated Google searching leading to top-ranking algorithmic fatwa websites.
   - *Barrier:* Algorithm surfaces diametrically opposed rulings (e.g., IslamQA Hanbali/Salafi strict prohibition vs. European Fatwa Council Maliki/Hanafi allowance) with zero contextual mediation or explanation of legal methodology (*Usul*).
   - *Consequence:* Spiritual scrupulosity OCD (*waswas*), cognitive paralysis, or cynical "fatwa shopping".

2. **`P-101` (First-Year Convert / Family Dilemmas & Sectarian Invalidation):**
   - *Persona:* Recent convert living in a non-Muslim household and Western social environment.
   - *Trigger:* High-stakes personal relationship dilemma (e.g., attending non-Muslim parents' funeral, Thanksgiving dinner, Christmas family gatherings, inheritance).
   - *Workflow:* Searches online for urgent, practical guidance; posts on convert forums.
   - *Barrier:* Complete absence of Madhhab/Ikhtilaf literacy. Scraped search algorithms present hyper-rigid, decontextualized verdicts as the sole "True Islam", invalidating the convert's instincts or prior guidance.
   - *Consequence:* Severe family estrangement, psychological alienation, trauma, and a 20% to 30% apostasy/disillusionment vulnerability within the first 3 years.

3. **`P-301` (Inquiring Believer / Algorithmic Fatwa Generation & Context Flattening):**
   - *Persona:* Any practicing Muslim querying conversational AI assistants or modern chatbots.
   - *Trigger:* Routine or urgent ritual/transaction question ("Does accidental bleeding break wudu?").
   - *Workflow:* Conversational inquiry into ChatGPT, Claude, or Islamic AI chatbots.
   - *Barrier:* Generative models flatten rich, conditional jurisprudence into rigid universal pronouncements ("Islam says X"), silently imposing one scraped school while erasing classical *Ikhtilaf* across the 4 Sunni madhahib and failing to ask clarifying questions about personal circumstance.
   - *Consequence:* Unqualified automated fatwas violating classical Islamic jurisprudence governance (`[NO AUTONOMOUS FATWA PERMITTED]`) and causing widespread intra-community friction.

*De-duplication Verdict:* **MAINTAIN AS 3 DISTINCT PROBLEMS.** `P-002` is an information retrieval/scrupulosity problem; `P-101` is an acute pastoral/convert retention crisis; `P-301` is an algorithmic governance and generative model failure.

### Cluster 3: Sacred Terminology, Localization & Curriculum Pedagogy
Three cards address Islamic language, conceptual understanding, and curriculum transmission:

1. **`P-102` (New Muslim Learner / Reading Jargon & Cultural Conflation):**
   - *Persona:* English-speaking adult convert, novice learner.
   - *Trigger:* Reading an introductory Islamic book, attending an English halqa, or browsing articles.
   - *Workflow:* Reading educational text containing dozens of untranslated Arabic loanwords (*Wudu*, *Ghusl*, *Taharah*, *Fard*, *Wajib*, *Sunnah Mu'akkadah*) without contextual scaffolding.
   - *Barrier:* Immigrant cultural customs (Desi, Arab, Somali food/clothing practices) are conflated with universal Islamic law (*Shari'ah*).
   - *Consequence:* Extreme cognitive exhaustion, feelings of cultural alienation, and stalled religious literacy.

2. **`P-201` (Volunteer Weekend Madrasah Teacher / Curriculum Franken-Assembly):**
   - *Persona:* Volunteer parent or university student teaching Sunday school (ages 6–14).
   - *Trigger:* Saturday night lesson preparation for Sunday morning class.
   - *Workflow:* Searching Pinterest, Google Images, and TeachersPayTeachers to assemble fragmented worksheets and coloring pages.
   - *Barrier:* Lack of developmental scaffolding; unvetted worksheets containing theological errors or dry rote memorization; high teacher turnover.
   - *Consequence:* High volunteer burnout (40–60% annual turnover); disengaged children who disconnect from the mosque during teenage years.

3. **`P-202` (Professional Translator & Publisher / Semantic Drift & Citation Loss):**
   - *Persona:* Bilingual Islamic translator, publisher, academic editor.
   - *Trigger:* Translating classical Arabic religious treatises into English or European languages.
   - *Workflow:* Working in translation environments (CAT tools, Word, DeepL/Google Translate).
   - *Barrier:* Neural machine translation flattens sacred theological polysemy (*Taqwa* -> "fear", *Bid'ah* -> "innovation", *Zuhd* -> "asceticism"); translation engines sever classical footnotes and citation links.
   - *Consequence:* Serious theological distortion; loss of academic provenance; 3x slower translation pace due to constant manual lexical verification across classical dictionaries (*Lisan al-Arab*).

*De-duplication Verdict:* **MAINTAIN AS 3 DISTINCT PROBLEMS.** Each addresses a different layer of knowledge transmission: learner comprehension (`P-102`), childhood classroom pedagogy (`P-201`), and professional scholarly translation (`P-202`).

### Cluster 4: Specialized Standalone Ritual Workflows
Two cards represent specialized, standalone sacred workflows with distinct physical and cognitive constraints:

1. **`P-003` (Quran Memorizer & Reciter / Mutashabihat al-Lafz Navigation):**
   - *Persona:* Quran memorizer (Hafidh), student, Taraweeh leader.
   - *Friction:* Verbal similarities (*Mutashabihat al-Lafz*) across disparate surahs cause severe memory lapses during recitation.
   - *Workflow Limitation:* Modern Quran apps (Quran.com, Tarteel) operate strictly as linear codices (Surah 1 -> Surah 114); they lack cross-canonical comparative views, forcing memorizers to rely on physical marginal notes or manual GoodNotes PDF screenshot workarounds.

2. **`P-100` (First-Year Convert / Solitary Salah Cognitive Overload):**
   - *Persona:* Adult convert in Days 1–90 of Islam.
   - *Friction:* Multi-modal sensory overload during solitary five daily prayers (simultaneously managing 17 motor transitions, unfamiliar Arabic phonetics, sequence rules, and wudu preservation).
   - *Pedagogical Deficit:* Existing tools present Salah as an all-or-nothing requirement from Day 1, ignoring the classical Prophetic principle of gradualism (*Tadrij*); converts pray with paper printouts or phones on the prayer mat, inducing deep guilt and motor instability.

*De-duplication Verdict:* **MAINTAIN AS STANDALONE PROBLEMS.** Both address foundational, daily acts of sacred devotion with unique physical and cognitive modalities.
