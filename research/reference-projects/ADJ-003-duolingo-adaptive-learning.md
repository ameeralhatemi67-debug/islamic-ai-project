## 4. Key Mechanisms to Transfer to Islamic AI
1. **Tiered Competency Progression for Ritual Devotion (`P-100`):**
   - Implement the classical 4-tier *Tadrij* prayer progression directly into software:
     - *Level 1 (The Prophetic Concession):* Physical movements + simple Tasbeeh (*SubhanAllah wa bihamdihi*).
     - *Level 2 (Foundational Recitation):* Surah al-Fatiha phonetics + basic postures.
     - *Level 3 (Core Liturgy):* Fatiha + short Surah + Tashahhud.
     - *Level 4 (Complete Sunnah):* Full adult prayer liturgy with optional invocations.
2. **Audio-First, Hands-Free Adaptive Guidance:**
   - EdTech tools use voice-activity detection (VAD) and audio cues rather than forcing users to touch screens mid-lesson.
   - *Transferable Mechanism:* Real-time audio coaching with smart pausing that adapts to the worshipper's physical pace without requiring them to glance at or touch a smartphone on the floor.
3. **In-Flow Progressive Terminology Tooltips (`P-102`):**
   - Duolingo lets users tap any foreign word in a sentence to see an instant, inline tooltip definition without leaving the exercise.
   - *Transferable Mechanism:* A browser reading extension or app reader that highlights Arabic loanwords in Islamic articles and opens a 3-tiered tooltip (plain English translation, legal status, cultural context) with zero context switching.
