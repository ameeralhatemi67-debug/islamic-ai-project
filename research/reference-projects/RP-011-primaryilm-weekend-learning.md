## 2. Basic Information
- **Project Names:**
  - **Weekend Learning Publishers:** https://weekendlearning.com (Physical curriculum textbooks)
  - **PrimaryIlm:** https://primaryilm.com (Printable Islamic worksheet portal)
- **Organization / Creators:** Commercial educational publishers based in the US and UK.
- **Launch Dates:** Weekend Learning (~2008); PrimaryIlm (~2019).
- **Current Status:** Both active and widely utilized across Western weekend schools.
- **Primary Language(s):** English.

---

## 3. Target User & Use Case
- **Target User:** Volunteer weekend school teachers, homeschool parents, and madrasah administrators.
- **Core Job:** "Prepare engaging, age-appropriate Islamic studies lessons and activities for Sunday school students aged 5 to 14."

---

## 4. Key Capabilities & Product Teardown
1. **Weekend Learning Publishers:**
   - Structured 10-level textbook series covering Islamic Studies, Qur'an, and Sirah.
   - Standardized chapters with end-of-chapter fill-in-the-blank questions and homework sheets.
   - Physical books purchased by mosques/parents annually.
2. **PrimaryIlm:**
   - Online library of 2,500+ downloadable PDF printables, board games, coloring sheets, and crafts.
   - Searchable by topic (e.g., 5 Pillars, Prophets, Ramadan).
   - Subscription-based pricing ($30–$50/year).

---

## 5. Known Limitations & Why P-201 Persists
1. **The Static PDF / Physical Print Trap:** Neither tool provides a dynamic or adaptive lesson planning workflow. A volunteer teacher preparing on Saturday night must still manually read chapters, estimate activity time budgets, and invent classroom engagement hooks from scratch.
2. **Pedagogical Rigidity of Textbooks:** Reading textbooks aloud in class leads to severe student disengagement and behavioral disruption in 2-hour weekend classes (`E03-003`).
3. **Severe Age-Bracket Gap:** PrimaryIlm is almost entirely designed for early childhood (ages 4–9); it offers virtually nothing for middle-school and high-school teenagers (ages 11–15) who struggle with complex identity and theological doubts.
4. **No Modular Scaffolding Engine:** Neither product adapts to classroom time constraints (e.g. "I have 40 minutes to teach Prophet Musa to 4th graders").

---

## 6. Strength Against Our Problem Space
- These tools represent the **exact fragmented status quo** that exhausts volunteer teachers. Weekend Learning forces rigid book reading, while PrimaryIlm forces ad-hoc worksheet hunting, leaving the core lesson planning toil completely unsolved.
