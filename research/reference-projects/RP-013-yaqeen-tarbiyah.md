## 2. Basic Information

- **Current Status:** Active, well-funded, premier intellectual think-tank for English-speaking Muslims.
- **Country / Region:** United States (Global reach).
- **Primary Language(s):** English.

---

## 3. Target User & Use Case
- **Target User:** Western Muslims, youth, college students, educators, and community leaders seeking intellectually rigorous responses to modern moral, philosophical, and theological doubts.
- **Core Job:** "Access academically sound, peer-reviewed Islamic research that bridges traditional orthodoxy with contemporary sociology, science, and philosophy."

---

## 4. Key Capabilities & Product Features
1. **Long-Form Academic Research:** Hundreds of comprehensive papers analyzing topics like evolution, LGBT issues, apostasy, convert integration, and slavery in Islam.
2. **Yaqeen Curriculum & Tarbiyah Project:** High-level educational frameworks for Islamic schools and youth groups, structuring character education, worldview formation, and moral development.
3. **Animated Infographics & Video Series:** Converts academic research papers into accessible YouTube videos and downloadable slide decks.

---

## 5. Known Limitations & Why Our Problems Survive
1. **High Conceptual Reading Barrier (`P-102`):** Yaqeen papers are written at an advanced undergraduate/graduate level (20–40 pages with hundreds of academic footnotes). For a newly converted Muslim or a novice seeker looking for a quick explanation of basic terms (*Wudu*, *Ghusl*, *Fard*), Yaqeen is completely overwhelming.
2. **Lack of Granular Lesson Plans for Volunteers (`P-201`):** The Tarbiyah Project offers macro-level curricular philosophies and thematic objectives, but does not provide a turnkey, 40-minute Sunday school lesson plan with activities and printables that an untrained volunteer can run on Sunday morning.
3. **Not an Interactive Fatwa or Takhrij Tool (`P-001`, `P-002`):** Yaqeen is an essay/research publishing house, not a dynamic Q&A engine, hadith verifier, or comparative fiqh portal.

---

## 6. Strength Against Our Problem Space
- Provides outstanding conceptual frameworks and theological content, but leaves the operational reading-aid (`P-102`) and weekly classroom assembly (`P-201`) workflows completely unaddressed.
