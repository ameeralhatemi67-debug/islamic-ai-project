## 4. Key Capabilities & Product Breakdown
1. **Namaz App & Step by Step Salat:**
   - Visual 2D illustrations or 3D character animations showing physical postures (Qiyam, Ruku, Sujud, Jalsah).
   - Audio recordings of recitations with phonetic transliteration and English translations.
   - Separate modules for Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha.
2. **Revertly:**
   - Dedicated "Path to Prayer" onboarding for converts.
   - Audio-guided lessons and a "prayer builder" tool.
3. **Pillars:**
   - Privacy-focused, beautifully designed prayer tracker and notification engine.
   - Tracks consistency and building prayer streaks, but deliberately delegates actual prayer coaching.
4. **My Salah Mat:**
   - Physical electronic mat with touch sensors under feet, knees, and forehead that plays audio recitations when pressed.

---

## 5. Teardown: Why P-100 (Salah Learning Overload & Lack of Gradualism) Survives
1. **Total Absence of Prophetic Gradualism (*Tadrij*):** Virtually all existing apps mandate full adult Arabic liturgy from Day 1 (17 postures, full Surah al-Fatiha, Tashahhud, Durood Ibrahim, Salams). They completely omit the authentic Prophetic concession documented in *Sunan Abi Dawud 858* and *Sunan an-Nasa'i 924* (`E02-002`), where a beginner who cannot recite the Quran is explicitly instructed to recite simple Tasbeeh (*SubhanAllah, Alhamdulillah, Allahu Akbar*) while learning the physical movements.
2. **The "Phone on the Rug" Disruption:** Users must place their smartphone on the floor in front of their prayer rug, squinting at tiny transliterations while in bowing or prostration. Screen timeouts, banner advertisements (Step by Step Salat), and the physical need to tap "Next" mid-prayer destroy spiritual focus and break prayer continuity.
3. **Rigid Pacing & Desynchronization:** Audio tutorials play at a fixed tempo. If a convert takes 3 extra seconds to position their limbs in prostration, the audio has already moved to the next movement, inducing panic and motor desynchronization.
4. **Physical Inflexibility of Hardware:** Products like *My Salah Mat* cost $70–$100, are non-portable, and cannot be used discreetly in corporate prayer rooms, university spaces, or travel.

## 6. Strength Against Our Problem Space
- These apps prove that millions of people search for prayer learning tools every year. However, their pedagogical rigidity and failure to provide hands-free, motion-adaptive gradualism directly generate the acute cognitive overload and guilt documented in `P-100`.
