## 2. Basic Information
- **Project Name:** IslamQA.org (formerly SunniPath / Darul Ifta Aggregator)
- **Organization / Creator:** Independent traditional Sunni portal aggregating fatwas from established Darul Ulooms and muftis.
- **Official URL:** https://islamqa.org
- **Launch Date:** ~2012
- **Current Status:** Active, large archive, frequently confused with IslamQA.info.
- **Primary Language(s):** English (primary), with citations in classical Arabic and Urdu.

---

## 3. Target User & Capabilities
- **Target User:** Traditional Sunni Muslims (predominantly Hanafi, Shafi'i, Maliki, Hanbali) seeking answers from verified Darul Ulooms (seminaries).
- **Core Features:**
  1. Aggregates over 100,000 fatwas from dozens of accredited institutions, including AskImam.org, Darul Ifta Deoband, Darul Ifta Birmingham, SeekersGuidance, MuftiOnline, and others.
  2. School Filter: Allows users to filter answers by school of thought (Hanafi, Shafi'i, Maliki, Hanbali).
  3. Seminary Citation: Every answer cites the originating seminary, authoring mufti, and date.

---

## 4. Known Limitations & User Friction
1. **Uncurated Collision of Contradictory Answers:** If a user searches "Is seafood halal in the Hanafi school?", IslamQA.org returns 12 different fatwa links from 5 different seminaries. Some say shrimp is permissible (*makruh tanzihi*), others say strictly impermissible (*makruh tahrimi*). The portal offers ZERO synthesis, explanation of why scholars diverged, or guidance on how a layperson should navigate the contradiction (`P-002`).
2. **Confusing Search Collisions:** Standard keyword search returns redundant, slightly varied duplicates from different years.
3. **Severe Brand Confusion:** Users constantly confuse `IslamQA.org` (traditional multi-madhhab Deobandi/Barelvi/Shafi'i aggregator) with `IslamQA.info` (monolithic Athari/Salafi site by Shaykh al-Munajjid).

---

## 5. Strength Against Our Problem Space
- Demonstrates the failure of raw, unmediated fatwa aggregation. Simply gathering links into a single database does not solve *Ikhtilaf* paralysis; it often amplifies it unless accompanied by structured comparative synthesis and *Usul* explanations.
