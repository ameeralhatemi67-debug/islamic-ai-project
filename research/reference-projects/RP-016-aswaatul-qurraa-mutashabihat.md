## 2. Basic Information
- **Project Names:**
  - **Mutashabihatul Quran (by Aswaatul Qurraa):** https://aswaatulqurraa.com/mutashabihat/ (Web database & PDF compilations)
  - **Ayaty (متشابهات القرآن الكريم):** Android / iOS mobile applications
  - **Mushaf al-Huffadh (Dar al-Salam):** Authoritative physical printed Mushaf with margin mutashabihat cross-references.
- **Launch Dates:** Various (2015–2024)
- **Current Status:** Active in specialized hifdh niches; widely circulated as static PDFs and mobile lists.
- **Primary Language(s):** Classical Arabic (primary); minimal English transliteration.

---

## 3. Target User & Capabilities
- **Target User:** Advanced Quran memorizers, Taraweeh imams, and Tahfeez teachers.
- **Core Features:**
  1. Static Indexing by Juz: Organizes similar verses by Juz number (e.g., "Juz 1 Mutashabihat with other Surahs").
  2. Textual Listing: Displays the ayah text alongside references to where similar wording appears in other parts of the Quran.
  3. Printed Marginal References: The physical *Mushaf al-Huffadh* prints small colored symbols and Surah/Ayah numbers in the margin beside repeated verses.

---

## 4. Why P-003 Persists Despite These Tools
1. **Zero Interactive Word-Level Diffing:** These platforms present whole verses as static paragraphs. The user must manually scan 30 Arabic words to spot the single preposition or letter that differs (e.g. *wa-sari'u* vs *sari'u*).
2. **No Interactive Audio Playback:** Unlike Quran.com or Tarteel, these specialized tools do not offer synchronized multi-qari audio playback to train the ear to hear subtle rhythmic and phonetic variations across the matched verses.
3. **Absence of Cognitive Scaffolding & Mnemonic Logic:** They list *what* is similar, but never explain *why* (e.g. the classical balaghah treatises of al-Iskafi or al-Kirmani explaining the theological rationale for word choice), leaving memorization as pure brute-force mechanical memorization.
4. **Outdated User Interfaces & Platform Silos:** Available as crude Android apps, raw web tables, or 300-page scanned PDFs that cannot be integrated into modern memorization routines.
