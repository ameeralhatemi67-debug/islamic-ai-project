## 1. One-Sentence Problem Statement
Non-Arabic-speaking newly converted Muslims struggle to perform their daily obligatory prayers (Salah) because existing educational materials and mobile apps enforce full adult Arabic recitation and multi-posture synchrony immediately from Day 1 without pedagogical gradualism (*Tadrij*), resulting in acute performance anxiety, feelings of invalidity, and prayer avoidance.

## 2. Primary User & Context
- **Primary User:** Newly converted Muslims (Day 1 through Month 6), non-Arabic speakers, primarily self-directed adult learners in Western or minority-Muslim settings.
- **Why This User Matters:** Salah is the foremost pillar of Islamic daily practice and the primary spiritual anchor post-Shahada. If the initial prayer-learning experience is paralyzed by overwhelming cognitive burden and guilt, converts disengage from religious practice early in their journey.
- **User Context & Trigger:** Immediately after uttering the Shahada, the new Muslim is told they are obligated to pray five times daily. Alone in their room, facing a prayer rug, they must execute 17 daily rak'ahs across five distinct prayer windows.
- **Environmental Constraints:** Solitary home environment, lack of in-person Arabic teachers, time pressure from prayer windows (e.g., Fajr before dawn), device placed awkwardly on the floor.
- **Existing Tools Used:** Smartphones open to YouTube tutorials or apps (e.g., NamazApp, Step by Step Salat), printed paper transliteration "cheat sheets" laid on the prayer mat, handwritten index cards.

## 3. Secondary Users & Stakeholders
- **Secondary Users:** Mosque educators, imams, and convert mentors who find themselves repeatedly coaching anxious converts through basic prayer anxiety.
- **Human Reviewers / Authorities Involved:** Imams, local teachers, and family members observing the new convert's practice.

## 4. Job to Be Done
- **When:** A newly converted Muslim faces the arrival of a daily prayer time (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha).
- **I want to:** Confidently execute a valid ritual prayer at my current capability level without panic, confusion, or feelings of spiritual failure.
- **So that:** I fulfill my obligation to Allah, experience spiritual tranquility, and build a sustainable habit of daily worship.
- **Success looks like:** Completing the prayer on time with calm presence of mind (*Khushu'*), knowing the prayer is valid according to authentic concessions, while steadily progressing in memorization week by week.

## 5. Current Reconstructed Workflow
- **Trigger:** Prayer time enters or user realizes they must pray before the time window expires.
- **Step 1:** User performs Wudu (often consulting an infographic or app to ensure steps are not missed).
- **Step 2:** User sets up physical workarounds: prints out transliteration cheat sheets, places their smartphone with an instructional YouTube video or app on the floor at the front of the prayer rug.
- **Step 3:** User attempts to pray: synchronizing physical movements (standing, bowing, prostrating, sitting) while squinting at the floor text, reading unfamiliar Arabic phonetics (*Ghayril maghdoobi 'alayhim...*), and attempting to keep track of the rak'ah count.
- **Step 4:** User loses their place, mispronounces a word, forgets which rak'ah they are on, or experiences device timeouts mid-prostration, causing intense doubt (*Waswas*) over whether the prayer was completely invalidated.
- **Final Outcome:** User finishes prayer feeling spiritually drained, anxious, and guilty rather than comforted, or abandons solitary prayers altogether until they can visit a mosque.

## 6. Workflow Evidence Grounding
- **Step 1 Status:** DIRECTLY OBSERVED (Abundant convert tutorials, infographics, and forum questions on Wudu sequence).
- **Step 2 Status:** DIRECTLY OBSERVED & USER REPORTED (Dozens of Reddit r/converts posts describing praying with phone/paper on the floor; E02-001).
- **Step 3 Status:** USER REPORTED (Documented convert testimonies detailing mental exhaustion from simultaneous motor-phonetic execution; E02-001).
- **Step 4 Status:** USER REPORTED & DIRECTLY OBSERVED (Frequent forum queries asking "Did I break my prayer if I stumbled on Fatiha?", "Is reading from phone valid?").
- **Inferred / Missing Workflow Steps:** The exact point where users transition from cheat sheets to full unassisted memory is poorly tracked and varies from 3 weeks to over a year.

## 7. Core Pain Point
- **Exact Friction Point:** Step 3 & 4 — The simultaneous execution of unfamiliar physical body postures, phonetic foreign-language recitation, rak'ah sequence tracking, and fear of procedural invalidation, executed without real-time adaptive feedback or pace control.
- **Root Cause Hypothesis:** Complete absence of pedagogical gradualism (*Tadrij*) in digital products. Modern tools present the prayer as an all-or-nothing adult liturgy (17 mandatory components), ignoring authentic classical juristic concessions (e.g., Sunan Abi Dawud 858, where the Prophet ﷺ permitted reciting Tasbeeh/Tahmeed for those unable to recite Qur'an) and lacking interactive multi-modal synchronization.

## 8. Consequences & Impact
- **Immediate Consequence:** Mental exhaustion, prayer delays, praying in a state of high anxiety rather than devotion (*Khushu'*), lost focus.
- **Long-term Consequence:** "Salah burnout" where the convert begins skipping prayers, feels like a "bad Muslim", and eventually disengages from practicing Islam.
- **Religious & Trust Consequence:** Adopting incorrect movements, intense scrupulosity/obsessive doubts (*Waswas*), or ungrounded beliefs that God rejects imperfect pronunciation.
- **Emotional / Cognitive Cost:** Imposter syndrome, shame, feeling completely inadequate compared to heritage Muslims.

## 9. Frequency & Severity
- **Frequency:** Multiple times daily (5 times every single day).
  - *Evidence & Confidence:* High (Salah is an invariant daily requirement; E02-001).
- **Severity:** High friction to Critical (blocks the central daily rite of the religion).
  - *Evidence & Confidence:* High (Universal convert testimony; E02-001).

## 10. Existing Workarounds
1. **Workaround 1 (Floor Cheat Sheet):** Printing large-font transliteration sheets or writing on poster boards placed on the floor before the prayer rug.
2. **Workaround 2 (Follow-Along Media):** Playing YouTube prayer tutorial videos or NamazApp audio at 0.5x speed on a smartphone propped up against a wall, attempting to mimic movements in real-time.
3. **Workaround 3 (Congregational Outsourcing):** Only praying when able to attend a mosque, following the Imam's movements so that personal recitation is minimized or waived.
- **Why Workarounds Fail:** Paper on floor causes neck strain, eye squinting, and raises theological anxieties about stepping on sacred verses. Video playback is rigid: if the user takes 2 seconds longer in prostration, the video advances to the next position, throwing the worshipper out of sync. Mosque attendance is impractical 5 times a day for working or isolated converts.

## 11. Initial Reference Candidates Encountered
- **Candidate 1:** Namaz App | Target: Reverts / Beginners | What it does: 2D visual illustrations with step-by-step audio recitation for all 5 prayers | Observed limitation: Static playback, cannot adapt to user's real-time movement, requires touching screen with hands during prayer, Hanafi-only postures without explaining variations.
- **Candidate 2:** Step by Step Salat (App) | Target: Beginners | What it does: Textual and audio guide for prayer movements and Wudu | Observed limitation: Dense walls of text, rigid pace, banner advertisements that disrupt focus during worship.
- **Candidate 3:** My Salah Mat / Tenfold Interactive Mat | Target: Children & Reverts | What it does: Touch-sensitive physical mat that recites audio when knees and feet touch pads | Observed limitation: Expensive physical hardware ($70–$100), fixed prerecorded pacing, socially awkward/impossible to travel with or use in public/workplaces.

## 24. AI Opportunity Evaluation (Agent 06 — Phase 4)
- **Assigned AI Opportunity ID:** AI-OP-004 (Privacy-Preserving On-Device Pose-Adaptive Salah Coach with Prophetic Tadrij)
- **Strongest Non-AI Baseline:**
  - Pedagogical gradualism curriculum (*Tadrij*): A structured 4-tier learning progression based on classical Prophetic sunnah (*Sunan Abi Dawud 858*):
    - Level 1: Physical postures + simple silent Tasbeeh (SubhanAllah, Alhamdulillah, Allahu Akbar).
    - Level 2: Adding Al-Fatiha in English/transliteration audio.
    - Level 3: Arabic Al-Fatiha + simple Tashahhud.
    - Level 4: Full adult prayer liturgy.
  - Timer-based hands-free audio pacing: Fixed-tempo audio cue player (e.g. playing posture changes every 6 seconds through a wireless Bluetooth earbud).
  - Physical floor guide / laminated prayer mat with color-coded footprints and postures.
- **Specific AI Capability Justified:**
  - **On-Device Computer Vision Pose Tracking (MediaPipe / CoreML Lightweight Skeletal Landmarking)**:
    - Fixed timer audio is rigid and fails in real worship: if a convert takes 3 seconds longer to transition into prostration (*sujud*) or pauses to remember a word, fixed audio continues ahead, inducing panic, broken focus (*khushu'*), and prayer abandonment.
    - A lightweight skeletal landmarking model running strictly on-device (via smartphone camera propped on a table) tracks joint angles (hip flexion, knee angle, head position) to detect the user's exact prayer posture transition (*Qiyam* $\rightarrow$ *Ruku* $\rightarrow$ *Qawmah* $\rightarrow$ *Sujud* $\rightarrow$ *Jalsah*).
    - Audio prompts adapt dynamically to the user's actual physical pace, waiting until the posture is physically achieved before whispering the corresponding dhikr/recitation.
  - **Voice Activity Detection (VAD) / Keyword Spotting (KWS)**: Detects when the user finishes reciting or whispers "Allahu Akbar" / "SubhanAllah" to trigger the next postural transition cue.
  - **Zero Generative LLM & Strict Offline Privacy**: 100% on-device edge processing. No video frames, audio recordings, or biometric data are ever sent to the cloud (safeguarding private prayer in home bedrooms).
- **Why AI May Help:**
  - Eliminates the cognitive desynchronization between mind, body, and phone screen. Converts no longer have to squint at paper cheat sheets on the floor or tap a screen mid-prayer with wet wudu hands.
  - Implements authentic Prophetic gradualism (*Tadrij*) as an active, responsive coaching loop rather than a passive, guilt-inducing checklist.
- **Why AI May Be Unnecessary or Inferior:**
  - Computer vision can be impeded by loose prayer garments (*thobes*, abayas), dim bedroom lighting, or camera occlusion.
  - Therefore, the system must include an **inferior but robust non-AI fallback**: audio-only accelerometer mode (phone in pocket or smartwatch wrist-tilt detection) or a single-tap audio pedal.
- **Expected Measurable Improvement:**
  - Motor-recitation synchronization breakdown: reduced from >70% (with YouTube/paper workarounds) to <10%.
  - Cognitive load during first 90 days of prayer (measured via NASA-TLX): >60% reduction.
  - Prayer retention in convert cohort: significant drop in prayer abandonment and spiritual burnout.
- **Technical Feasibility & Data Constraints:**
  - *Data Availability & Models:* High. Google MediaPipe Pose and Apple Vision framework provide pre-trained, production-ready, highly optimized on-device 33-point skeletal landmark models that run at 30+ FPS on mid-tier mobile CPUs with zero cloud dependencies.
  - *Feasibility:* High. Prototype can be built using standard Flutter/React Native on-device vision plugins.

## 25. Adversarial Red Team Review (Agent 07 — Phase 5)
- **Assigned Challenge ID:** RT-P-100 (Physical Garment Occlusion & Prayer Surveillance Anxiety)
- **Critical Assumptions Challenged:**
  1. *Computer Vision Feasibility Assumption:* Assumes MediaPipe 33-point skeletal landmarking can accurately track joint angles in real-world prayer conditions where users wear loose Islamic clothing.
  2. *Worship Psychology Assumption:* Assumes a vulnerable, self-conscious new convert is comfortable setting up a smartphone camera in their bedroom or living room to film their solitary prayers.
  3. *AI Necessity Assumption:* Assumes computer vision is necessary when an adaptive audio pacing earbud or a simple, unobtrusive Bluetooth single-tap ring/pedal solves hands-free synchronization without cameras.
- **P0 Failure Modes (Fatal Flaws):**
  - **P0-1 (Physical Garment Occlusion & Environmental Collapse):** Skeletal tracking algorithms (MediaPipe Pose) fundamentally depend on visible limb contrast to infer joint coordinates (hips, knees, ankles, elbows). Islamic prayer strictly mandates covering the *'Awrah* with loose-fitting garments: women pray in flowing abayas, long prayer skirts (*isdal*), and head coverings; men often wear loose *thobes* or wide trousers. Loose fabric completely obscures knee flexion and hip joints. Crucially, during prostration (*Sujud*), the body folds into a compressed, self-occluding shape where standard 2D/3D skeletal models lose tracking entirely. Furthermore, small convert living spaces lack the 6-to-8-foot optical clearance needed for a propped phone camera to capture both standing (*Qiyam*) and ground prostration (*Sujud*) in a single frame. The computer vision architecture will fail in the wild.
- **P1 Issues (Severe Complications):**
  - **P1-1 (Spiritual Incompatibility & Surveillance Anxiety):** Prayer (*Salah*) demands absolute spiritual surrender and inner stillness (*Khushu'*). Pointing a camera lens at oneself during solitary worship induces acute self-consciousness, performance anxiety, and psychological unease, destroying the sacred contemplative state—regardless of marketing assurances regarding on-device processing.
  - **P1-2 (Ritual Invalidation via Excessive Movement / Harakah Kathirah):** If the pose tracker misdetects a transition or freezes, the convert is forced to glance at the screen, adjust the phone, or step out of the prayer orientation (*Qiblah*). In classical Islamic jurisprudence, three consecutive extraneous movements (*Harakah Kathirah*) or turning away from the Qiblah invalidates the prayer entirely (*Fasad al-Salah*).
- **Theological & Ethical Risks:**
  - *Techno-Fetishization of Worship:* Transforming the most sacred direct communion between creature and Creator into a computer-vision tracked biomechanical exercise.
  - *Invalidation of Sacred Rites:* Algorithmic misfires causing repetitive invalid prayer cycles and spiritual frustration.

## 26. Cross-Examination & Surviving Claims (Phase 6)
- **CX Reference ID:** CX-P-100 (Adjudicated under CX-DOCK-03 from RT-P-100)
- **Key Objections Debated:**
  1. *Physical Garment Occlusion & Environmental Collapse (P0):* Islamic prayer mandates loose-fitting garments (*abayas*, *isdals*, *thobes*) that physically obscure skeletal joint contrast (hips, knees). During prostration (*Sujud*), the body collapses into a self-occluding shape where standard 2D/3D skeletal landmarking loses tracking completely. Standard bedrooms lack the 6–8 foot optical clearance required to frame both standing and prostration without wide-angle distortion.
  2. *Spiritual Incompatibility & Surveillance Anxiety (P1):* Solitary prayer requires absolute spiritual surrender and contemplation (*Khushu'*). Setting up a camera to record one's worship induces performance anxiety and acute self-consciousness, destroying the spiritual state of prayer.
  3. *Ritual Invalidation via Extraneous Movement (P1):* In Islamic jurisprudence across all four Sunni schools, three consecutive extraneous movements or turning away from the Qiblah invalidates prayer (*Fasad al-Salah*). Glance-checking or adjusting a glitching phone camera directly invalidates the convert's prayer.
- **Accepted Limitations & Scoped Boundaries:**
  1. *Camera Pose Tracking Abandoned:* The smartphone camera computer vision pipeline (MediaPipe 33-point skeletal landmarking) is **PERMANENTLY TERMINATED AND DISCARDED** due to physical garment occlusion, optical constraints, and worship surveillance anxiety.
  2. *Architectural Pivot to Audio & Motion Coaching:* The project is strictly reframed as an **Adaptive Hands-Free Audio & Motion Coach**:
     - Primary interaction mode: Voice Activity Detection (VAD) / whisper detection (detecting completions of "Allahu Akbar" or silent pauses) delivering whispered prompt cues through a single wireless Bluetooth earbud.
     - Secondary motion mode (optional / non-optical): Low-friction device orientation (accelerometer/gyroscope in pocket or smartwatch wrist-tilt) or a discreet single-tap Bluetooth ring/floor pedal.
  3. *Zero Biometric Capture:* Complete elimination of cameras guarantees 100% sacred privacy in personal domestic prayer spaces.
- **Surviving Claims:**
  1. *Gradualism (*Tadrij*) Problem Validity:* The underlying problem—cognitive overload, motor-recitation desynchronization, and total lack of gradualism for Days 1–90 converts—is fully validated, urgent, and unaddressed by existing static checklist apps.
  2. *Adaptive Pacing Superiority:* Dynamically waiting for the worshipper to finish reciting before cueing the next transition fundamentally outperforms rigid fixed-tempo timers, eliminating panic and prayer abandonment.
  3. *Prophetic Pedagogy Soundness:* The 4-tier Prophetic *Tadrij* curriculum (*Sunan Abi Dawud 858*) provides an unimpeachable, scholar-approved pedagogical framework.
