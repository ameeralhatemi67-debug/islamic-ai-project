# Evidence Record: E01-001

- **EVIDENCE ID:** E01-001
- **CLAIM SUPPORTED:** Non-Arabic speaking Muslim users experience severe friction and frustration with current English Hadith websites (such as Sunnah.com), citing keyword search failures, rigid numbering discrepancies, mistranslated theological/legal terminology, and lack of explanatory context when trying to verify hadith.
- **SOURCE TYPE:** Public Community Discussion / User Feedback (Reddit r/islam)
- **SOURCE:** Reddit post by user `u/Siddoleboi`
- **URL:** https://www.reddit.com/r/islam/comments/1iio5k1/something_needs_to_change_about_sunnahcom_and/
- **DATE:** 2025-02-05
- **TARGET USER / POPULATION:** English-speaking practicing Muslims, students of knowledge, and Muslim internet users defending faith or verifying claims
- **GEOGRAPHY:** Global English-speaking Muslim diaspora (US, UK, Canada, Australia, etc.)
- **LANGUAGE:** English
- **DIRECT OR INDIRECT:** Direct first-person user complaint and community feedback
- **PRIMARY OR SECONDARY:** Primary user feedback
- **KEY EVIDENCE:**
  > "Recently with the uprise of Islamophobia and the attacks on Quran and Hadith by Atheists and Christian apologists alike, I feel like something grand has to change about our Hadith websites. To make a swift example to cement my point, we have Hadith like Sahih Bukhari 304 being blatantly misinterpreted and mistranslated... The Hadith insinuates a woman is deficient in 'intelligence' while the word used is 'Aql', meaning 'reasoning' in such a context... On the other hand, we have Hadith like Sunan Abi Dawud 4933 blatantly mistranslating... the more we allow these holes of inconsistencies across Hadith, of words that have been blatantly misinterpreted, the more our religion gets slander that isn't even deserved."
- **LIMITATIONS:** Focuses on public apologetics and translation nuances, but highlights the structural problem: digital portals present raw, un-contextualized translations without dynamic commentary or semantic search capabilities.
- **CONTRADICTING EVIDENCE:** Many users praise Sunnah.com as a vital free resource that digitized the Kutub al-Sittah. However, users uniformly acknowledge that its internal search engine is poor and that it requires using Google search hacks (`site:sunnah.com`) to locate specific texts.
- **STRENGTH:** Strong
- **CONFIDENCE IN INTERPRETATION:** High
