# Evidence Record: E04-006

- **EVIDENCE ID:** E04-006
- **CLAIM SUPPORTED:** Major contemporary Islamic fatwa institutions (IslamQA, Islamweb, Dar al-Ifta al-Misriyyah) have issued authoritative rulings establishing that AI is legally and theologically disqualified from acting as an autonomous mufti, deriving rulings (*ijtihad*), or issuing personalized fatwas, permitting AI solely as an assistive research retrieval tool under strict human scholarly verification.
- **SOURCE TYPE:** Islamic Institutional Sources / Published Fatwas
- **SOURCE:** IslamQA (Fatwas on Artificial Intelligence and Fatwa Issuance); Islamweb (Fatwa Center on AI in Shari'ah Rulings); Dar al-Ifta al-Misriyyah (Official conference declarations and rulings on AI and Fatwa).
- **URL:** https://islamqa.info/en/answers/
- **DATE:** 2023?2025
- **TARGET USER / POPULATION:** Muslims worldwide querying digital platforms for personal legal and religious decisions.
- **GEOGRAPHY:** Global (covering prominent Salafi, Azhari, and traditional institutional bodies).
- **LANGUAGE:** Arabic and English
- **DIRECT OR INDIRECT:** Direct formal fatwa rulings.
- **PRIMARY OR SECONDARY:** Primary institutional rulings.

## KEY EVIDENCE
1. **Theological Ineligibility (*Faqd al-Ahliyyah*):** AI systems lack the essential legal faculties required for issuing rulings: *malakat al-fiqh* (jurisprudential aptitude), understanding of *maqasid al-shari'ah* (higher objectives of Islamic law), and *fiqh al-waqi'* (comprehension of the questioner's personal, social, and psychological reality).
2. **Absence of Accountability (*Adam al-Mas'uliyyah*):** A religious ruling is a sacred trust (*amanah*) carrying accountability before Allah. An automated statistical model cannot bear legal or moral responsibility for leading a user into sin or harm.
3. **Prohibition of Direct Lay Reliance:** Institutions strictly forbid ordinary Muslims from accepting or acting upon AI-generated answers for personal halal/haram verdicts, divorce, marriage, contracts, or ritual validity without consulting a qualified human scholar.
4. **Assistive Exception for Qualified Researchers:** Scholars permit AI strictly as a mechanical assistant (for keyword search, text retrieval, or indexing), on the strict condition that the researcher possesses the qualifications to independently verify the underlying primary texts.

## LIMITATIONS
- Contemporary institutional consensus is reactive to early generative AI systems (ChatGPT 3.5/4); future specialized RAG systems with verified human-in-the-loop audit may receive narrower, tailored authorizations for educational indexing.

## CONTRADICTING / DISCONFIRMING EVIDENCE
- Several state fatwa councils (e.g., UAE Fatwa Council, Dar al-Ifta al-Misriyyah) are actively developing internal AI assistants; however, these tools are strictly constrained as administrative triage or retrieval aids for human muftis, not autonomous public-facing decision makers.

## STRENGTH & CONFIDENCE
- **STRENGTH:** Strong (Cross-institutional consensus spanning diverse theological and geographical centers).
- **CONFIDENCE IN INTERPRETATION:** High.
