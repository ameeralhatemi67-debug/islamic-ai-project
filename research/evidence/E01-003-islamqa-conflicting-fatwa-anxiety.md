# Evidence Record: E01-003

- **EVIDENCE ID:** E01-003
- **CLAIM SUPPORTED:** Practicing Muslims seeking practical religious answers via general search engines encounter context-blind, single-madhhab fatwas that induce acute religious anxiety, guilt, obsessive-compulsive scrupulosity (*waswas*), and decision paralysis due to unmediated conflicting rulings.
- **SOURCE TYPE:** Public First-Person Testimony & Community Response (Reddit r/islam)
- **SOURCE:** Reddit post by user `u/Fail_Exact`
- **URL:** https://www.reddit.com/r/islam/comments/16i9hrw/has_anyone_else_gone_through_islamqa_ruining/
- **DATE:** 2023-09-14
- **TARGET USER / POPULATION:** Practicing everyday Muslims, working professionals, university students, and youth
- **GEOGRAPHY:** Global diaspora / English-speaking Muslims
- **LANGUAGE:** English
- **DIRECT OR INDIRECT:** Direct first-person user experience and extensive corroborating comments (126+ comments)
- **PRIMARY OR SECONDARY:** Primary user testimony
- **KEY EVIDENCE:**
  > "I was one time wanting to cut down on watching some tv shows that were probably haram and decided to do a google search and ended up on islamqa. Unfortunately this created a downward spiral. I am now so worried over everything being haram and halal and worrying about making Allah angry with me if I don’t follow. I’m so exhausted this whole obsession with haram and halal has made me INSANE and made me question to leave Islam because everything I’m doing is basically haram and I don’t want to leave Islam. I love it! But this is so strict and a lot of the scholars and websites it’s making me insane. I can’t do anything. It makes me feel like I’m doomed and it’s making me miserable and having literal panick attacks. Has anyone suffered with this?"
  > 
  > Corroborating community comment: "People cannot live with the guilt."
- **LIMITATIONS:** Describes an intense psychological response (religious scrupulosity), but the underlying mechanism is universal: web search aggregates isolated fatwas from strict textualist sources without contextualizing juristic principles (*Usul*), legitimate school differences (*Ikhtilaf*), or practical gradualism (*Tadrij*).
- **CONTRADICTING EVIDENCE:** Many users appreciate IslamQA.info for its evidence-dense, uncompromising references to classical Athari/Hanbali texts.
- **STRENGTH:** Strong
- **CONFIDENCE IN INTERPRETATION:** High
