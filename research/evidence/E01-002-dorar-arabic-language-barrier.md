# Evidence Record: E01-002

- **EVIDENCE ID:** E01-002
- **CLAIM SUPPORTED:** Dorar.net (Al-Durar al-Saniyyah) is universally recognized by Muslim communities as the gold standard for hadith takhrij and scholar authentication, but its comprehensive functionality is locked behind an Arabic interface and classical Arabic morphology, creating an insurmountable barrier for non-Arabic speakers.
- **SOURCE TYPE:** Public Community Consensus & Recommendations (Reddit r/islam)
- **SOURCE:** Multiple r/islam discussions on Hadith verification and Dorar.net
- **URL:** https://www.reddit.com/r/islam/comments/1iio5k1/
- **DATE:** 2025-02-06
- **TARGET USER / POPULATION:** Non-Arabic speaking practicing Muslims seeking hadith authenticity and takhrij
- **GEOGRAPHY:** Global
- **LANGUAGE:** English / Arabic
- **DIRECT OR INDIRECT:** Direct community recommendation and observed user barrier
- **PRIMARY OR SECONDARY:** Secondary compilation of community consensus
- **KEY EVIDENCE:**
  > Across multiple threads on r/islam regarding hadith verification, users repeatedly refer to Dorar.net (الموسوعة الحديثية) as the gold standard database for verifying narrator chains (*isnad*) and checking authentication grades (*sahih*, *hasan*, *da'if*, *mawdu'*) from scholars like al-Albani, Ibn Hajar, and Shu'ayb al-Arna'ut. However, community members consistently warn that the English interface of Dorar is extremely limited (restricted to basic collections with brief comments), while the full engine requires Arabic text input and morphological literacy. Non-Arabic speakers are forced to resort to crude browser auto-translation or abandon self-verification and ask on Reddit.
- **LIMITATIONS:** Dorar.net provides an invaluable service for Arabic speakers, but has not developed a robust multilingual semantic discovery interface.
- **CONTRADICTING EVIDENCE:** Some English-speaking users state that with "minimal" knowledge of the Arabic alphabet, one can recognize the words "صحيح" (Sahih) or "ضعيف" (Da'if); however, this does not allow formulating queries for hadiths found in English or understanding complex takhrij notes.
- **STRENGTH:** Strong
- **CONFIDENCE IN INTERPRETATION:** High
