- **EVIDENCE ID:** E01-005
- **CLAIM SUPPORTED:** Quran memorizers (Huffadh) are forced to invent cumbersome manual workarounds—such as using third-party PDF editing apps to screenshot, annotate, and color-code similar verses across distant Surahs—because existing digital Quran applications lack non-linear comparative navigation for *Mutashabihat*.

- **SOURCE:** Reddit r/Hifdh comment in thread `t3_1rlqyy3`
- **URL:** https://www.reddit.com/r/Hifdh/comments/1rlqyy3/struggling_with_surah_isra_and_surah_taha_in/
- **DATE:** 2026-03-05
- **TARGET USER / POPULATION:** Quran memorizers (Huffadh), advanced Hifdh students, and reciters
- **GEOGRAPHY:** Global Muslim community
- **LANGUAGE:** English / Arabic
- **DIRECT OR INDIRECT:** Direct first-person user demonstration of an elaborate workaround
- **PRIMARY OR SECONDARY:** Primary user workaround
- **KEY EVIDENCE:**
  > "It won’t let me post pictures but ever since I started using a pdf editing app and I manually went and identified and labeled all similar verses , this problem has gotten much easier.
