# Evidence Record: E01-006

- **EVIDENCE ID:** E01-006
- **CLAIM SUPPORTED:** Quran memorizers leading prayers (especially Taraweeh in Ramadan) experience severe anxiety, cognitive confusion, and recitation breakdown ("jumping across surahs") when encountering *Mutashabihat* (similar verses) without structured comparative tools during revision.
- **SOURCE TYPE:** Public First-Person Testimony (Reddit r/Hifdh)
- **SOURCE:** Reddit post by user `u/Cheetah_Hunter97` on r/Hifdh
- **URL:** https://www.reddit.com/r/Hifdh/comments/1rlqyy3/struggling_with_surah_isra_and_surah_taha_in/
- **DATE:** 2026-03-05
- **TARGET USER / POPULATION:** Huffadh, prayer leaders (imams), and Quran students preparing for public recitation
- **GEOGRAPHY:** Global
- **LANGUAGE:** English / Arabic
- **DIRECT OR INDIRECT:** Direct first-person user struggle
- **PRIMARY OR SECONDARY:** Primary user testimony
- **KEY EVIDENCE:**
  > Title: "Struggling with surah isra and surah taha in taraweeh (mutashabihat)"
  > Post Body: "Everytime those surahs get me so bad...i keep jumping all over the place it just gets super frustrating. How do you manage not to slip into other similar verses? Jazakallah."
  > 
  > Corroborating comment: "Ayah 60, ends with summa ata. I keep saying summa'atu saffa, wa qad aflahal yauma....similar things keep happening and frustrates me."
  > Another commenter: "I struggled with Namal Qasas and Isra this Ramadan Taraweeh mostly in Qasas."
- **LIMITATIONS:** High-intensity during Ramadan/Taraweeh, but affects regular year-round revision for anyone memorizing the Quran.
- **CONTRADICTING EVIDENCE:** Highly experienced Qaris who have revised for decades report fewer slips, but acknowledge that mutashabihat remains the primary source of error throughout a hafidh's life.
- **STRENGTH:** Strong
- **CONFIDENCE IN INTERPRETATION:** High
